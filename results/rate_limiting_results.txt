Rate Limiting vs Model Extraction
=============================================
Attacker tries to send 8,000 queries; fidelity measured on 2,000 hold-out images

No defense: queries answered=8000  requests refused=0  copy fidelity=98.95%
Label only: queries answered=8000  requests refused=0  copy fidelity=98.90%
Limit 500 queries: queries answered=500  requests refused=75  copy fidelity=94.75%
Limit 500 queries + label only: queries answered=500  requests refused=75  copy fidelity=93.60%
